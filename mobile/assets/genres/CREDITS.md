[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free for apps, no permission needed).
Each one was checked: free (not Unsplash+), no recognizable faces, no logos or brand names.

They were made smaller (600 × 300) and given a dark fade at the bottom so the genre names are easy to read.
